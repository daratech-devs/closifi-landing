Where it goes wrong

Finding them was the hard part. Forgetting them is the expensive part.

Nobody decides to abandon a lead. Reps get busy, the reminder slides, and a month later that name is dead weight in a spreadsheet. Here are the four places it happens.

LEAK 01

Captured, never entered

A rep finds ten good prospects on LinkedIn, saves them to a doc, and means to add them to the CRM later. Later doesn't come.

LEAK 02

Contacted twice, then dropped

Most replies arrive after the fourth or fifth touch. Most reps stop at the second, because the follow-up lives in their memory instead of a system.

LEAK 03

Replied, nobody noticed

The answer lands in a different inbox from the tool that sent the message. By the time someone spots it, the prospect has moved on.

LEAK 04

Interested, never booked

"Sounds good, send me some times." Four emails later, the calendar tennis has killed whatever momentum there was.

Straight answers

The awkward questions, answered before you ask.

You are going to compare us against cheaper tools. Here is where we are genuinely different, and where we are genuinely not the right choice.

Why per user, when other tools charge one flat fee?

Because the tools charging one flat fee are sending tools. You pay for volume, everyone shares one login, and there is no record of who owns which relationship.

Closifi holds the relationship, not just the send. Each person gets their own prospect list, their own call log, their own permissions and their own history on every lead. If all you need is to blast email from a shared account, a flat-fee sending tool will be cheaper — and you should buy that instead.

Other tools say "unlimited leads". Why do you have a limit?

Storage is cheap and we treat it that way: your allowance grows with every seat, archived and unsubscribed leads never count, and more can be added for $10 a month per 25,000.

What we meter is new leads added each month, because that tracks the work the system actually does for you. We would rather show you a number you can see and manage than advertise "unlimited" and enforce it quietly through a fair use policy.

What happens when I hit a limit?

Nothing dramatic. You get a warning at 80% and 90% with a breakdown of what is using your allowance. At the limit, new leads pause — nothing is deleted, nothing is locked, and your sequences keep running.

Then you choose: archive cold leads for free, add capacity, or move up a plan. The free option is always shown first.

Where do the leads actually come from?

From you. Closifi generates leads by capturing them — one click from a LinkedIn profile, a bulk import, or the API — and then finds and verifies the email address. What it isn't is a database you buy access to and search through.

If you want to filter 200 million contacts by job title, buy a data provider. If you want the prospects you've picked yourself worked properly and never dropped, that's this.

Questions

The things buyers ask us first.

What exactly counts as a "new lead"?

Any person added to your workspace who wasn't there before — from the Chrome extension, a CSV import, the API, or typed in by hand. Every source counts the same way. Updating, enriching or re-syncing someone you already have doesn't count, and neither does a duplicate.

How does Closifi compare to a cold email tool?

A cold email tool is built to send at volume and get into the inbox. Closifi is built to work a prospect all the way to a booked meeting — who owns them, what stage they're at, what was said on the call, when to try again. Sending is one part of that rather than the whole product.

Plenty of teams run both. If you only need sending, buy the sending tool.

Am I locked into a contract?

On monthly billing, no — cancel whenever you like and you won't be billed again. Annual plans run for the year you've paid for. Enterprise agreements are negotiated per contract. There's no cancellation fee on any plan and no penalty for downgrading.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, at any time, on every plan including the trial. Export leads, activity history and campaign results to CSV yourself from inside the product — you don't need to ask us, and exports aren't held back during a cancellation.

How does the Chrome extension work with LinkedIn?

It captures the profile details already visible to you on a page you're viewing and saves them to your CRM. It works at human pace and doesn't run bulk automated scraping in the background.

You're responsible for using it within LinkedIn's terms and your own local data protection rules. If you're unsure how that applies to you, check with your legal advisor before rolling it out across a team.

Do I need Twilio to make calls?

Calling on Pro and Enterprise runs through Twilio, and you connect your own account. Your numbers and call records stay yours, and you're billed at cost for the telephony plus Closifi's $0.02 per outbound minute. Twilio isn't needed for anything else in the product.

Can we move our existing CRM data across?

Yes. Every new workspace gets a free one-time migration import — 10,000 leads on Starter, 20,000 on Growth, 60,000 on Pro, and no cap on Enterprise. It doesn't touch your monthly allowance. If your data is messy or spread across several systems, we'll help you map it during onboarding.

What does the free trial include?

Seven days, three seats and 250 lead captures — enough to import your leads, capture new ones and get a live sequence running. No card required, and nothing bills automatically when it ends.

Seven days is tight for seeing replies come back, so if you're mid-test when it runs out, ask and we'll extend it.
